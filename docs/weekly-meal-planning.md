# 🗓️ Weekly & Monthly Meal Planning

FamilyPlates makes scheduling meals fast, collaborative, and visual.

---

## 🍽️ The Planning Matrix

The Meal Planner supports both a **Weekly Grid** and a **Monthly Calendar Matrix**:

* **Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Slots:** Each day has dedicated slots for all 3 meals.
* **1-Tap Recipe Assignment:** Pick from your household recipe collection or enter a custom meal (e.g. *"Leftovers"* or *"Dining Out"*).
* **Chef / Cook Assignment:** Assign who is cooking each meal with their personal avatar badge and initial.
* **Notes & Sides:** Add notes like *"Defrost chicken morning of"* or side dishes.
* **Edit & Reschedule Meals:** Hover over any planned meal and click the ✏️ pencil icon to modify the dish, reassign the cook, change notes/times, or reschedule to a different date or meal time with instant Hotwire Turbo Stream updates. Calendar subscriptions automatically reflect rescheduled meals and cook updates on their next refresh.

---

## 📅 Live Calendar Subscriptions

Click **"Subscribe"** in the planner header to connect live calendar feeds:
* **Whole Household Feed:** See the entire family's meal schedule in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, or mobile devices.
* **My Cooking Shifts:** Filter the calendar feed to only meals where you are assigned as the cook.
* **1-Tap Setup:** Instant 1-tap subscribe buttons for Apple Calendar (iOS/macOS) and Google Calendar on the web, plus universal `.ics` link copying.

---

## 😋 Family Cravings & Voting

Family members can request their favorite recipes for the upcoming week:
* Cravings appear in a dedicated toolbar above the planner.
* 1-click drag/schedule into any open slot.

---

## 🖨️ High-Contrast Fridge Printouts

Click **"Print Fridge Sheet"** from the planner to generate clean, ink-friendly 1-page refrigerator schedules:

* **Weekly View (`/meal_plans/:id/print?view=week`):**
  * Clean table layout with days as columns and meals as rows.
  * Clear cook names and prep/cook durations.
  * Letter landscape format styled for standard home printers with zero browser margins clipping.
* **Monthly View (`/meal_plans/:id/print?view=month`):**
  * 1-page full-month calendar grid with days constrained to the active month.
  * Clean dividers and readable meal names.
