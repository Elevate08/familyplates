# 🍳 Recipes & Pantry Management

FamilyPlates includes tools to import recipes from the web, categorize ingredients by supermarket aisle, and maintain a smart pantry inventory.

---

## 🌐 1-Click Recipe Web Scraper

* Import recipes directly by pasting a URL (supports all Schema.org / JSON-LD recipe websites).
* Automatically extracts title, description, prep/cook times, servings, instructions, ingredients, and thumbnail images.

---

## 🛒 Aisle-Organized Grocery List

The Grocery List aggregates all ingredients required across scheduled recipes for the week:

* **Supermarket Aisle Categorization:** Grouped by Produce, Meat & Seafood, Dairy & Eggs, Bakery, Pantry & Grains, Frozen, Spices & Seasonings, and Other.
* **Unit Normalization & Aggregation:** Automatically sums matching ingredients (e.g. `2 tbsp olive oil` + `3 tbsp olive oil` = `5 tbsp olive oil`).
* **Mobile-Friendly Checklist:** Check off items while walking the aisles with instant strike-through and progress counter.

---

## 🛡️ The Pantry Shield

Never double-buy staples you already have in your kitchen:

* Mark kitchen essentials (e.g., *Salt, Pepper, Olive Oil, Flour, Soy Sauce*) as **Staples** in your Pantry inventory.
* When viewing your weekly grocery list, pantry staples are highlighted with the **Pantry Shield** badge or excluded so you only shop for what's actually needed.
