# FamilyPlates: The 5-Minute Family Meal Planner

## Brand Identity
**FamilyPlates** — *"What's on our plates this week."*
A warm, playful, family-first meal planning application that turns the daily question of *"What's for dinner?"* into a frictionless, 5-minute collaborative routine.

## Problem Statement
How might we enable a busy household to collaboratively plan meals in under 5 minutes, producing a beautiful fridge-ready calendar, an automated calendar feed, and an aisle-organized mobile grocery list with zero friction or clutter?

## Core Touchpoints
1. **The Fridge:** Single-sheet `@media print` physical menus (weekly & monthly layouts) for glanceable kitchen viewing.
2. **Personal Devices & Calendars:** Zero-overhead tokenized `webcal://` `.ics` feed syncing meal schedules straight into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.
3. **The Grocery Store:** Responsive mobile checklist organizing ingredients by supermarket aisle with smart "Pantry Shield" staple highlighting.

## User Experience Highlights
- **Who's in the Kitchen? (Profile Picker):** Family members choose their avatar profile upon login to submit dish cravings and customize page theme colors.
- **Admin PIN Protection:** Optional 4-digit security PIN for parents/organizers.
- **Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Scheduling:** Complete meal coverage with assigned chefs.
- **10 Starter Family Recipes & Rich Web Scraping:** Pre-loaded family classics and JSON-LD web scraper for instant recipe imports.
- **Smart Pantry Emojis:** Visual grocery aisles and pantry management with automatic emoji identification.
